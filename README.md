# GeoMap Arbitrage

**Open-source local economic opportunity intelligence.**

GeoMap Arbitrage maps lawful, executable economic mismatches to the people and places that can exploit them: unmet local demand, skill scarcity, geographic price spreads, underused assets, logistics gaps, remote-income/local-cost advantages, and other forms of real-world arbitrage.

The core question is deliberately practical:

> Given this person, in this place, with these skills, resources, constraints, and time horizon: what can they actually do to build income, why should it work, what would it cost, and what evidence supports the recommendation?

This repository is being rebuilt as a fresh consumer of [`Spectating101/citation-engine`](https://github.com/Spectating101/citation-engine). GeoMap owns economic semantics and scoring. Citation Engine owns inspectable evidence/basis lineage, decisions, authority, and receipts.

## Status

`v0.1` seed under active development. Initial fixtures are synthetic; they demonstrate the engine contract and **must not be interpreted as live market opportunities**.
