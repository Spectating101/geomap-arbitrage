# Safety and boundaries

GeoMap is economic decision support, not a guarantee of income and not a substitute for legal, tax, licensing, financial, or professional advice.

## Required behavior

- treat revenue/cost as uncertain ranges;
- preserve source dates and confidence;
- distinguish observed, verified and estimated/modelled evidence;
- require local legal/regulatory review where status is unknown or permit-bound;
- account for working capital, transaction friction, defects, returns, transport and time where relevant;
- expose research gaps instead of fabricating certainty;
- block explicitly prohibited catalog activities;
- minimize personal data;
- prefer tiny reversible tests before large capital commitment.

## Out of scope

The initial project does not attempt to optimize or facilitate:

- illegal markets or regulatory evasion;
- deceptive pricing, fraud or impersonation;
- exploitation of vulnerable people;
- harassment/spam targeting of mapped businesses or individuals;
- insider/non-public information;
- guaranteed-return investment schemes;
- automatic execution of financial-market trades;
- unsafe physical activities merely because a spread appears profitable.

## Synthetic fixtures

All initial example opportunities are fictional. They exercise code paths; they are not claims about any real location or market.
